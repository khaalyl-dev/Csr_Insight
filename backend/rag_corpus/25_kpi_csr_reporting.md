# KPIs, reporting, and CSR documentation (RAG)

## KPIs and measurable outcomes

A useful CSR KPI is **measurable**, has a **clear baseline or target**, and a **reporting period** (monthly, quarterly, annual). Prefer units that match the activity (tonnes CO2 avoided, hours volunteered, beneficiaries reached).

When USER_DATA gives live counts, those override any generic KPI wording here.

## Reporting and validation

Corporate workflows often include **draft → submitted → validated** for plans. Activities can move from **draft** through **in progress** to **completed** or **validated**. Rejected items should record a short reason where the product supports it.

## User guidance

Users review lists in the app with **filters** (year, status, site). If totals in the assistant differ from the screen, remind them to clear filters or check planned vs realized views.

## Privacy

Documentation in this corpus is generic. **Never** invent passwords, tokens, or personal data; live answers must follow **USER_DATA** from the server.
