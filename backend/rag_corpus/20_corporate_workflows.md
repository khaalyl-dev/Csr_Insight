# Corporate user workflows

Corporate users (CORPORATE_USER) typically validate CSR plans and activities submitted by sites, manage the user directory, import consolidated Excel reports where enabled, and review audit history.

**Global vs scoped corporate access:** some corporate accounts see all sites; others are limited to a subset of sites. The live USER_DATA section states which site codes and aggregates apply to the signed-in user.

When explaining validation steps, mention that the exact buttons and labels may depend on language (French/English) and on the current status of each plan or activity.
