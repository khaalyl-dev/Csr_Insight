# CSR plans and activities

A **CSR plan** belongs to a site and a calendar year. Plans move through workflow states (e.g. draft, submitted, validated) according to your organization’s process.

**Planned activities** are line items under a plan: they describe what the site intends to do (category, dates, budget, etc.). **Realized activities** record what was actually executed, with evidence such as photos or documents.

Corporate users may see plans for many sites; site users only see plans for sites they are assigned to. The application enforces the same visibility rules in the UI and in API responses.
